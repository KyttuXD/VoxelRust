# Planes - a mod that comes with the game, in its own folder.
#
# A small plane. Set it down on flat ground, board it, and hold forward to
# take off; it glides down when you let go.
# Nothing here is part of the program: a vehicle is a `vehicle` block naming
# a model, what it travels on (water, rail, land or air) and its numbers.
# Change them or add your own (docs/DATA.md). Switch the mod off in
# Settings > MODS, or delete its folder, and these are gone from the game.
model plane file models/plane.bbmodel
texture icon_plane paint wings 206 52 48

vehicle plane
  name Plane
  model plane
  icon icon_plane
  medium air
  speed 22
  height 1.3
  width 1.8
  seat 1.2

craft plane 1 iron_ore 10 planks 8 white_wool 4
