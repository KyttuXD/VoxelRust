# Natural Disasters - a mod that comes with the game, in its own folder.
#
# Earthquakes, tornadoes and tsunamis, written here rather than in the
# program: each `disaster` line says how often one may come, how long it
# lasts, what must be true for it to start, and the chat commands carried
# out a few times a second while it lasts. Change them, or add your own
# (see docs/MODDING.md). Switch the mod off in Settings > MODS (or delete
# its folder) and none can happen; with it on they still only happen when
# NATURAL DISASTERS is on in Settings > GAME.

sound quake effects 1.20 0.95 40 30 0.03 1.00 An earthquake
sound twister effects 1.60 1.00 100 100 0.10 0.70 A tornado howls

# About every twenty minutes the ground shakes for seven seconds...
disaster earthquake every 1200 lasts 7 then tsunami do /shake 0.35; /sound quake
# ...and by the sea, a wave follows: the water climbs three blocks, holds
# and drains away. (No `every`: it never comes on its own.)
disaster tsunami lasts 24 if coast do /flood 3
# Within a couple of minutes of a storm over open, low country, a funnel
# crosses close by, dragging you in and tearing up plants and leaves.
disaster tornado every 120 lasts 26 moves 3.5 funnel if storm and lowland and biome plains savanna do /pull 14 2.2; /strip 3 40; /sound twister

when disaster earthquake do /title EARTHQUAKE
when disaster tsunami do /title TSUNAMI - GET TO HIGH GROUND
when disaster tornado do /title TORNADO
