# Natural Disasters - a mod that comes with the game, in its own folder.
#
# Earthquakes, tornadoes and tsunamis. Nothing about them is in the
# program: each `episode` line says how often one may come, how long it
# lasts, what must be true for it to start, and the chat commands carried
# out a few times a second while it lasts. Change them, or add your own
# (see docs/MODDING.md). Switch the mod off in Settings > MODS (or delete
# its folder) and none can happen; with it on they still only happen when
# NATURAL DISASTERS (the `setting` line below) is on in Settings > GAME.

setting disasters off Natural disasters

sound quake effects 1.20 0.95 40 30 0.03 1.00 An earthquake
sound twister effects 1.60 1.00 100 100 0.10 0.70 A tornado howls
sound hail effects 0.25 0.90 3000 4200 0.45 0.45 Hail rattles down
sound blizzard effects 2.00 0.70 180 260 0.30 0.50 A blizzard wails

# About every twenty minutes the ground shakes for seven seconds...
episode earthquake every 1200 lasts 7 then tsunami if setting disasters do /shake 0.35; /sound quake
# ...and by the sea, a wave follows: the water climbs three blocks, holds
# and drains away. (No `every`: it never comes on its own.)
episode tsunami lasts 24 if coast do /flood 3
# Within a couple of minutes of a storm over open, low country, a funnel
# crosses close by, dragging you in and tearing up plants and leaves.
episode tornado every 120 lasts 26 moves 3.5 funnel if setting disasters and storm and lowland and biome plains savanna do /pull 14 2.2; /strip 3 40; /sound twister

# In a storm, hailstones come down for twenty seconds, and each one stings.
episode hailstorm every 300 lasts 20 if setting disasters and storm do /hurt 1; /sound hail
# Cold country in a storm: the snow takes the strength out of you (hunger drains).
episode blizzard every 600 lasts 40 if setting disasters and storm and biome tundra taiga do /feed -1; /sound blizzard

when episode earthquake do /title EARTHQUAKE
when episode tsunami do /title TSUNAMI - GET TO HIGH GROUND
when episode tornado do /title TORNADO
when episode hailstorm do /title HAILSTORM - FIND COVER
when episode blizzard do /title BLIZZARD - KEEP WARM
